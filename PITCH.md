# Website pitch: Brew & Bloom, Church Street, Bengaluru

> Adjust the [brackets] after you meet the owner. Keep it to one page.

## Who the business is
Brew & Bloom is a café on Church Street, Bengaluru, serving specialty coffee, cold brew, matcha, snacks and desserts. Its customers are [students, office workers, shoppers and tourists] who [stop by for a quick coffee or a longer catch-up].

## The problem
Someone searching on their phone for a café near Church Street cannot easily see Brew & Bloom's menu, prices, opening hours or location in one place. [Describe what is missing today: no website, only Instagram, outdated hours.] People who cannot find answers quickly choose another café, and staff spend time on the phone answering the same questions.

## The solution
A fast, mobile-friendly website with:
- the full **menu and prices**, always up to date
- a **drink builder** so customers can design their coffee, with oat, coconut or almond milk, and see the price instantly
- a **drink recommender** that helps first-time visitors choose
- **online ordering** that sends the order straight to the café on WhatsApp, for pickup or dine-in
- a **rewards programme** (points, tiers and a customer dashboard) that brings people back
- hours, a Google Map, **Call**, **WhatsApp** and **Get directions** buttons

## How it helps the café grow
- **More customers:** people find the café on Google and Maps, see the menu and get directions in seconds.
- **More orders and bigger orders:** easy ordering and add-ons (extra shot, syrups, plant-based milks) lift the average bill.
- **More repeat visits:** points, tiers and one-tap reorder give regulars a reason to return.
- **Less work for staff:** menu, hours and location answer themselves.
- **More trust:** a polished website makes the café look established.
